### Design Language ###
A design language is a cohesive set of rules, styles, and visual elements—such as colors, shapes, typography, and interactive behaviors—that guide how products or spaces are built. It acts as a unified vocabulary, ensuring that different items or screens share a recognizable, harmonious identity.


### Wedding Photos ###
Wedding Photo Structure

The wedding memory board should organise photographs around the story and emotional progression of the wedding day, rather than treating it as a generic photo gallery.

Wedding Day Photo Categories

Wedding photographs can typically include:

Getting Ready
    Hair and makeup
    Getting dressed
    Bridesmaids and groomsmen
    Parents and family
    Wedding details such as rings, shoes, flowers and invitations
    Candid moments before the ceremony
First Look / Before the Ceremony
    Couple seeing each other for the first time
    Reactions
    Guests arriving
    Wedding party
    Venue and decorations
The Ceremony
    Walking down the aisle
    Partner's reaction
    Vows
    Ring exchange
    First kiss
    Signing the register
    Walking back down the aisle
    Guest reactions
    Couple leaving the ceremony
    Confetti
    Cheers
    Hugs and congratulations
Family & Group Photos
    Immediate family
    Extended family
    Wedding party
    Friends
    Different combinations of guests
    Couple Portraits
    Formal portraits
    Natural and candid portraits
    Venue/location photographs
    Golden-hour or sunset photographs
Drinks Reception
    Guests chatting
    Drinks and canapés
    Candid interactions
    Games and activities
    Couple mingling with guests
Wedding Breakfast / Dinner
    Table and room details
    Couple entering
    Guests eating and drinking
    Candid table moments
Speeches
    Parent speeches
    Maid of honour / bridesmaid speeches
    Best man speeches
    Couple's speeches
    Laughter and emotional reactions
Cake Cutting
    Cutting the cake
    Couple reactions
    Guests watching
First Dance
    Couple's first dance
    Close emotional moments
    Guests watching
    Parent dances where applicable
The Party
    Dancing
    Friends and family celebrating
    Dance floor
    DJ or band
    Drinks
    Funny candid moments
    Evening portraits
    Night-time couple photographs
    Sparklers
    Fireworks
    Late-night dancing
Little Moments
    Emotional reactions
    Hugs
    Friends laughing
    Children playing
    Grandparents and family
    Unexpected or funny moments
    Behind-the-scenes moments
    Moments the couple may not have personally witnessed
